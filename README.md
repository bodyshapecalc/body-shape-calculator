# Body Shape Calculator — Free Tool for Men & Women

A free body shape calculator that identifies your body type from bust, waist, high hip, and hip measurements instantly. Works for both women and men. No sign-up required.

🔗 **Live Tool: [bodyshapecalc.com](https://bodyshapecalc.com)**

---

## What Is This?

[BodyShapeCalc](https://bodyshapecalc.com) is a free measurement-based body shape calculator. It uses the proportional relationships between your body measurements to identify your body shape — the same method used by fashion stylists and clothing designers.

All calculations run in your browser. No data is stored. No sign-up needed.

---

## Body Shapes Identified

The calculator identifies 7 main body shape types:

| Body Shape | Key Characteristics |
|---|---|
| Hourglass | Bust and hips balanced, waist 25%+ smaller |
| Pear | Hips 5–15% wider than bust |
| Triangle | Hips 15%+ wider than bust |
| Apple | Waist 90%+ of bust and hips |
| Rectangle | Bust and hips balanced, soft waist contrast |
| Inverted Triangle | Shoulders or bust 5%+ wider than hips |
| Spoon | Hips wider than bust with pronounced hip shelf |

---

## Measurements Used

- **Bust or Chest** — Fullest part of the chest
- **Waist** — Narrowest torso point
- **High Hip** — 3–4 inches below natural waist (optional but recommended)
- **Full Hips** — Widest point of hips and seat
- **Shoulders** — Optional, improves accuracy for inverted triangle
- **Height and Weight** — Used in body measurement calculator

Accepts both **inches** and **centimeters**.
